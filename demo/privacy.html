Privacy Policy for NEGIAO's WebGIS

Effective Date: August 14, 2026 (生效与更新日期：2026年8月14日)

NEGIAO's WebGIS ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our WebGIS platform and sign in with Google OAuth 2.0.

1. Information We Collect (信息收集与数据权限)

When you sign in to NEGIAO's WebGIS using Google OAuth 2.0, we only request access to basic identity information with your explicit consent:

  • OpenID: Used to uniquely identify your user profile within our platform.
  • Email Address (`email`): Used for account identity verification and communication.
  • Basic Profile (`profile`): Includes your display name and profile picture to display your account status.

2. Purpose of Data Usage (信息使用目的)

We request and use your Google account data solely for the following purposes:

  • To create and manage your personal user account on NEGIAO's WebGIS.
  • To save, bind, and sync your created WebGIS map projects, layer configurations, and spatial analysis history across devices.

3. Data Protection and Sharing (数据保护与非共享承诺)

  • No Third-Party Sharing: We strictly do NOT sell, rent, trade, or share your Google user data with any third parties or commercial entities.
  • Security: All communication and data transfers are encrypted using HTTPS/TLS protocols.

4. Data Deletion and Account Removal (数据删除与注销)

You have the right to revoke access to our application at any time. If you wish to delete your account or saved GIS projects from NEGIAO's WebGIS, please contact the developer.
