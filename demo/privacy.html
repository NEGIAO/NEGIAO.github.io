NEGIAO's WebGIS Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how NEGIAO's WebGIS collects, uses, and protects your information when you access our web application and authenticate using Google OAuth 2.0.

1. Information We Collect

When you authenticate with Google OAuth 2.0, we request explicit permission to access the following basic information:

  • OpenID: Used to uniquely identify your user profile within NEGIAO's WebGIS.
  • Email Address (`email`): Used for user login verification and account management.
  • Basic Profile (`profile`): Includes display name and profile picture to customize your user interface.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your Google account data exclusively for the following purposes:

  • To establish and manage your personal user account on NEGIAO's WebGIS.
  • To save, bind, and synchronize your WebGIS map projects, layer configurations, and spatial analysis records.

3. Data Protection and Non-Sharing Commitment

  • No Third-Party Sharing: We do NOT sell, rent, trade, or share your Google user data with any third parties or advertisers.
  • Encryption: All data transmissions are securely encrypted using standard HTTPS/TLS protocols.

4. User Data Control and Account Deletion

You may revoke authorization for NEGIAO's WebGIS at any time through your Google Account settings. If you wish to delete your saved map projects or user account from our system, please contact the application administrator.
